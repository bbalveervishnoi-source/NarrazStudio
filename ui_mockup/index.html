WPF XAML Design System Tokens for NarrazStudio

This design specification translates directly into MainWindow.xaml and App.xaml resources to replace the previous high-contrast blue accents with the modern Visual Studio Code 2026 frosted-glass and low-contrast flat aesthetic.

1. Theme 1 (Deep Black) Color Tokens

<!-- Deep Black Palette (#121314 & #191A1B) -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="AppCanvasBackgroundDark" Color="#121314"/>
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="SidebarBackgroundDark" Color="#191A1B"/>
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="CardBackgroundDark" Color="#161718"/>
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="CardBorderDark" Color="#222426"/>
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="SliderTrackDark" Color="#2D3035"/>
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="SliderFilledDark" Color="#6B7280"/> <!-- Monochromatic, NO bright blue -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="SliderThumbDark" Color="#F3F4F6"/>

<!-- Morphic Glaze Selected Overlay (Active State) -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="MorphicGlazeDarkFill" Color="#14FFFFFF"/> <!-- 8% white -->
<SolidColorBrush x:Key="MorphicGlazeDarkBorder" Color="#24FFFFFF"/> <!-- 14% white -->
