# Narraz Studio - Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** May 2026

## 1. Overview
At Narraz Studio, your privacy is our highest priority. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices regarding data collection, processing, and usage. By using the Narraz Studio application, you agree to the terms described in this policy.

## 2. No Data Collection
**Narraz Studio does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.** We do not track your usage patterns, nor do we require you to create an account, log in, or provide any identifying information to use the application. The app does not include any analytics or telemetry trackers.

## 3. Local Processing
All audio generation, text parsing, and file merging processes (including the use of the bundled FFmpeg binaries) occur **100% locally** on your device. Audio files are saved directly to your chosen local directory, and your narration history is stored only in a local SQLite/JSON file on your own hard drive. We do not have access to your generated audio files or your narration history.

## 4. Third-Party Services
Narraz Studio acts as a graphical interface for the Microsoft Edge TTS API. To generate speech from your text, the application transmits your text input securely to Microsoft's Text-to-Speech servers. 
- **No text is saved by us** or associated with your identity.
- We strongly recommend reviewing Microsoft's Privacy Statement regarding their online services.

## 5. Security
Because all file handling and storage occurs locally on your machine, the security of your data depends on your own device's security measures. Narraz Studio runs securely within the Windows environment and only requests network access to reach the required TTS servers.

## 6. Changes to this Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any updates will be reflected in the GitHub repository for this project.
