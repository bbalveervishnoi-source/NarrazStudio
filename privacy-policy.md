# Narraz Studio - Privacy Policy

**No Data Collection**: Narraz Studio does not collect, store, or share any personal user data.

**Local Processing**: All text-to-speech processing and file merging (FFmpeg) happen locally on your device.

**Third-Party Services**: This app uses Microsoft Edge TTS services. To generate speech, text is transmitted to Microsoft's servers. No text is saved or associated with your identity.
